# AI Survivors

A couch co-op party game: a multiplayer parody of *Vampire Survivors* where software engineers battle feature requests, bugs and managers — and ultimately AI itself. Up to eight friends play on one shared screen (a laptop or TV) and each uses their own phone as the controller: scan a QR code, pick a hand-drawn character and steer with your thumb.

The whole team shares one XP bar and votes together on upgrades at every level-up, surviving sprint after sprint of surprise events on the way to a final boss. Retro pixel art, chiptune-style sound and in-jokes for anyone who has ever sat through a stand-up.
