# AI Survivors

**A couch co-op party game where software engineers fight off the end of the world — or at least the end of the sprint.**

A multiplayer parody of *Vampire Survivors*. Up to eight friends play on one shared screen — a laptop or a TV — and every player uses their own phone as the controller. No extra gear, no accounts, no downloads.

## The game

- **Join in seconds.** Scan the QR code on the big screen, pick your fighter from a hand-drawn roster, and use your thumb as the joystick.
- **Survive the sprint.** Wave after wave of feature requests, bugs, and managers close in. Your weapons fire on their own — your job is to dodge, collect, and stay alive.
- **Level up together.** The whole team shares one XP bar. Every level-up, everyone votes on which upgrade to take.
- **Expect the unexpected.** Each sprint brings its own twist, with surprise events mid-round to keep everyone on their toes.
- **Beat the final boss.** It's been building up all game. It's AI.

Retro pixel art, chiptune-style sound effects, and plenty of in-jokes for anyone who's ever sat through a stand-up.

## Try it

Run `npm start` on a laptop, then open the address it prints on the big screen. Everyone else joins from their phone on the same WiFi.
